About us

A family of engineers, building things that last.

Satsang Technology grew out of years of building real, working software — and a belief that the best technology is the kind people can quietly rely on.

Our story

From building for our own community, to building for yours.

We started the way a lot of the best software starts — by solving our own problems. Building tools for our community, systems for the organisations we worked with, apps for real needs we saw around us.

Over the years that turned into a body of work: the operational systems that keep a large media business running, community and matrimony platforms, and apps published on the Play Store. Software that's been in production long enough to prove it works.

Satsang Technology brings that experience together as a studio — a family-run team taking on software, web, and mobile projects for others, with the same care we put into our own.
