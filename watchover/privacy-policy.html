Watchover

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy applies to the Watchover Android app, package com.satsang.watchover, developed by Satish Ratanchand Ranka, Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. It is published on the Satsang Technology website and describes the app's current data practices. WishApp has a separate privacy policy.

Face checks happen on the phone. Watchover uses front-camera photos to check whether the approved child is using the phone. Watchover does not upload these photos or face templates.

Child profiles and screen-time records stay in encrypted app storage. Google ML Kit, used for face detection, collects technical diagnostics separately, as explained below.

1. Parental supervision and children's information

Watchover helps parents and guardians supervise a child's screen time. An adult authorized to supervise the child sets up profiles, enrolls faces and approves timed sessions. Children are the subjects of these checks, so this policy explicitly covers their information.

Explain the camera checks to your child before use. Only enroll and supervise a child you are authorized to supervise, and obtain any consent required for your circumstances. Watchover is not intended for covert monitoring of adults or other people.

2. Information stored on the phone

Watchover stores the following in encrypted app storage:

  • Child profile names and enrolled face templates.
  • Daily screen-time allowances, bedtime settings and usage records.
  • Approved-session details and monitoring status.

A face template is a numerical representation of facial features used to compare the current viewer with the child selected for the session. Watchover has no cloud account, cloud backup or remote parent dashboard, and does not send these records to a publisher-operated server.

3. Camera checks and temporary photos

With consent and camera permission, Watchover takes front-camera photos during face enrollment and periodically during approved screen-time sessions, including while other apps are open. A visible notification indicates that monitoring is active.

Photos are processed on the device and deleted after processing. They are not retained as a photo history. If an operation is interrupted, temporary files may remain in private app cache until cleared by the operating system or removed when app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Watchover does not upload face photos or templates, sell them or use them for advertising. It does not record audio, read messages or contacts, track location or collect browsing history.

4. Permissions and phone authentication

  • Camera: enrolling faces and checking the approved child during a session.
  • Notifications and foreground service: making active monitoring visible while it runs.
  • Device Admin: requesting Android's phone lock when the session expires or protection detects a problem.
  • Phone authentication: verifying access to parent controls using the phone's own supported biometric or screen-lock credential.

Android handles phone authentication. Watchover receives the result, not your phone's PIN, password or system biometric templates. Anyone able to authenticate with the phone can authorize parent controls.

Watchover also reads relevant permission status, screen activity, battery and thermal conditions, and system clocks to operate sessions and adjust camera-check intervals.

5. Google ML Kit and other service providers

Google ML Kit performs on-device face detection. Its SDK collects technical diagnostics and usage metrics, including device and app information, installation identifiers, processing performance, API configuration and errors. These metrics are separate from Watchover's face photos and enrolled templates. Transmitted SDK metrics are encrypted using HTTPS. This version has no SDK-diagnostics opt-out setting.

See Google's ML Kit data disclosures, ML Kit terms and Privacy Policy for its practices. Google manages the retention and handling of data it receives under its applicable terms and policies.

Watchover has no advertising, in-app purchases, publisher-operated analytics endpoint or separate crash-reporting service in this version. Google Play and Android may separately process installation, security and diagnostic information under their own policies.

6. Your controls, retention and deletion

  • Stop monitoring: verify with your phone and use “End session & stop monitoring” in parent controls.
  • Delete a child: remove their profile in parent controls to delete that child's current face templates, profile and associated usage from Watchover's active storage.
  • Remove all local data: deactivate Watchover's Device Admin permission in Android settings, then uninstall the app. Clearing the app's data also removes its local records.
  • Revoke camera permission: change camera access in Android settings. During an active session, detected permission loss blocks the session.

Profiles, templates, settings and usage remain until deleted through these controls. Current-session and health records are overwritten as the app runs. Android application backup is disabled. Deleting local app data does not itself delete technical metrics already received by Google.

We cannot remotely access or delete the child profiles or templates held on your phone. Contact us if you need help using the deletion controls. If you email us, we receive the address and information you provide through our email service. We use it to respond and retain it only as needed for the request and any applicable obligations.

7. Security and recognition limits

Face templates and other app records use encrypted storage; temporary camera images are kept in private app cache during processing. System authentication guards parent controls. No security measure can guarantee protection against device compromise or operating-system behavior outside the app's control.

Face matching can make mistakes, including confusing similar-looking siblings, and is not resistant to photo spoofing. Checks are periodic, not continuous. Watchover is a supervision aid; uninstalling, force-stopping or removing permissions can bypass protection on an unmanaged phone.

8. This website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading it sends ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address, browser information, requested page and request time, to GitHub so it can deliver and secure the page. This page does not include advertising or analytics scripts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about the hosting provider's practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its “Last updated” date when our practices change. Material new uses of personal information will receive appropriate notice and, where required, consent before they begin.
